## Overview
I had the opportunity to host a workshop for beginners and chose surface analysis as the theme. Participants learned how to use analysis tools and how to approach a binary, and at the end solved a CTF-style binary challenge on their own.

## Key work
- Learn the basics of analysis tools
- Experience how to approach a binary
- Take on a CTF-style binary challenge at the end

## Notes
The content is aimed at beginners. I was glad the participants seemed to enjoy it.
